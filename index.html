boca

You talk, therefore I am.

What it is

Comments on any web page, in a Chrome side panel. Open it on a page and you see what people said about that page, and you can answer.

There is no boca server and no account. Comments live on public Nostr relays, so anyone with a Nostr client can read and answer them too.

How it works

  • A page is its address, without the #fragment.
  • A comment is a standard Nostr web comment, so other apps that write them show up in boca, and boca's show up in them.
  • Your key is made in the browser the first time you open the panel. It signs your comments, and you can back it up or use a signer extension instead.
  • Muting an author follows your key to other devices and other Nostr apps.

The README has the details and the limits, and PRIVACY.md says what leaves your computer.

Try it

Build it and load it into Chrome 123 or later, as the install steps say.
